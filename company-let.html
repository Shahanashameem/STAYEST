THE COMPANY LET MODEL

A professional alternative to traditional letting.

A company let is an agreement where the landlord grants a company the right to occupy or operate the property under agreed contractual terms.

Instead of your agreement being directly with an individual occupant, STAYEST becomes your corporate contractual partner.

We then manage the agreed operational responsibilities associated with the property, helping create a more structured relationship between the property owner and the operator.
