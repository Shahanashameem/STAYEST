THE STAYEST MODEL

What does Guaranteed Rent mean?

Guaranteed rent is a rental arrangement where the commercial terms are agreed in advance between the landlord and STAYEST.

Rather than relying on individual tenancy cycles, landlords work with a company as their contractual partner, with the agreed rental payment structured for the term of the agreement.

The property is then professionally operated and managed by our team in accordance with the agreed terms.
